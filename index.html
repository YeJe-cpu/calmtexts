After a fight

I've been thinking about earlier. I don't want to be right, I want us to be okay. Can we talk tonight?

Puts the relationship ahead of winning.
Where is this going

I really enjoy what we have, and I'm starting to catch feelings. I'd love to know where you're at.

Shares first, then asks. Feels safe to answer.
Left on read

I noticed my last couple of messages didn't get a reply. I'm not upset, I just don't want to keep texting into a void. Are you still interested?

Asks for a straight answer, calmly.

What this is (and isn't)

  • It is a shortcut for saying what you mean, kindly and clearly, when your brain goes blank.
  • It isn't a script to make anyone like you, come back, or change. Nothing can do that, and we won't pretend otherwise.
  • No manipulation. No mind games, no "make him chase you" tricks. Just honest words you can send without regret.
