After a fight: going first

What to text after a fight (and go first without losing)

Going first after a fight isn't losing. It's choosing the relationship over the scoreboard. These texts open the door without pretending the problem wasn't real.

3 texts you can send tonight

Text 1

I've been thinking about earlier. I don't want to be right, I want us to be okay. Can we talk tonight?

Puts the relationship ahead of winning.
Text 2

I need a little time to cool off so I don't say something I don't mean. Can we talk tomorrow?

A pause that sounds like care, not punishment.
Text 3

I hate when we're off like this. I'm ready to listen whenever you're ready to talk.

Soft, and it hands him the next step.

Before you hit send

  1. Own your part, not his. "I'm sorry for my part" invites him to own his.
  2. If you're still hot, ask for time instead of texting the essay you'll regret.
  3. Move the real talk off text. Tone gets lost on a screen.

Questions

Who should text first after a fight?

Whoever is calm first. Going first doesn't mean you were wrong; it means you want to fix it.

How long should I wait to text after an argument?

Long enough that you can write without heat. A few hours is common; just say you need the time so the pause doesn't read as punishment.
