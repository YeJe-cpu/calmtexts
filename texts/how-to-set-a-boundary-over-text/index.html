How to set a boundary over text (kindly)

A boundary is about what you'll do, not what he must do. Say it once, calmly, and mean it. Kind and clear can live in the same sentence.

3 texts you can send tonight

Text 1

I'm not comfortable with that. I'm telling you because I want this to work.

Clear no, framed as investing in the relationship.
Text 2

When plans change last minute, I feel like an afterthought. Can we give each other a heads-up next time?

Says how it lands and asks for one specific change.
Text 3

I'm happy to talk about this, but not while we're raising our voices. Let's pause and come back in an hour.

Protects the conversation, not just you.

Before you hit send

  1. Say how it lands, then ask for one specific change.
  2. Keep it short. Long explanations sound like you're asking permission.
  3. Follow through. A boundary you don't keep is just a suggestion.

Questions

Is it okay to set a boundary over text?

Yes, especially for small ones. For big ones, text to open the conversation and talk it through in person.

What if he gets upset about my boundary?

Some discomfort is normal. If he keeps pushing past it, that tells you something important.
