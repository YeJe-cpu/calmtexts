When his texts slow down

What to text him when he goes quiet

Replies got shorter. Gaps got longer. You're rereading old messages trying to work out what changed. The urge is to send something that gets a reaction. A better move: name the change once, kindly, and let his answer tell you what you need to know.

3 texts you can send tonight

Text 1

Hey, I've noticed we've been talking a little less lately. No pressure at all, just checking in. How are you, really?

Names the change without accusing him of anything.
Text 2

Totally fine if you're busy right now. Just let me know if something's changed on your side. I'd rather know than guess.

Gives him an easy way to be honest.
Text 3

I don't want to read into the quiet, so I'm asking instead: are we good?

Short, direct, and calm. One clear question.

Before you hit send

  1. Send one text, not a string of them. One message is a door; three is a wall.
  2. Skip the guilt line ("I guess I'm not a priority"). It invites defending, not honesty.
  3. Then go live your evening. How he replies, and how long it takes, is information.

Questions

Should I text him if he's been quiet for days?

Yes, once. A short, warm check-in that asks a real question is fine. Repeated follow-ups usually make the distance bigger.

What if he doesn't reply at all?

Silence after a clear, kind message is an answer. You can close the loop with dignity instead of chasing.
